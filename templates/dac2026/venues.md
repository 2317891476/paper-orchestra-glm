# Target Venues for Literature Search

Prioritize papers from these EDA / design-automation and architecture venues (DAC's community):

## Design Automation & EDA
- DAC (Design Automation Conference)
- DATE (Design, Automation & Test in Europe)
- ICCAD (International Conference on Computer-Aided Design)
- IEEE TCAD (Transactions on Computer-Aided Design)
- IEEE TVLSI (Transactions on VLSI Systems)
- ACM TODAES (Transactions on Design Automation of Electronic Systems)

## Verification & Test
- DVCon, MTV (Manufacturing Test), IEEE VTS
- FMCAD (Formal Methods in Computer-Aided Design)

## Architecture (when relevant)
- ISCA, MICRO, HPCA, ASPLOS

## ML for EDA
- relevant ML venues when the work is ML-for-hardware (NeurIPS/ICML workshops, arXiv cs.AR)

## Preprints
- arXiv (cs.AR, cs.ET, stat.ML when relevant)
